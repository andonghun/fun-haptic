01 — HARDWARE

Three DualSense features.

The controller features used here, and where each one is used.

01

HD haptics

Over USB the controller is a 4-channel audio device. Channels 3 and 4 drive the left and right grip.

02

Adaptive triggers

R2 can click, get heavier, vibrate or stop partway. Each weapon uses one of these.

03

Controller speaker

Small sounds such as reloads, shell casings and coins play from the controller. Everything else plays on the PC.

Experiences

Eight experiences.

All free and run in the browser. The previews use the same engine as the game.

HD hapticsAdaptive triggersSpeaker

Playground

Walk, run and jump on a wooden deck and a brick floor. Hit straw dummies with a sword, hammer, spear or fists, or shoot them with a pistol, SMG or bow.

  • Footsteps on the side of the foot that lands
  • Combos with Square, heavy attack with R2
  • Seven weapons on L1 and R1
Play
HD hapticsSpeaker

Weather lab

Twelve kinds of weather. Rain, snow and wind each vibrate differently. R2 sets the intensity.

  • Rain, storm, snow, blizzard, fog and more
  • Lightning on Triangle in rain and storm
  • Gusts move from one hand to the other
Play
HD hapticsSpeaker

Treasure tray

Tilt the controller to roll a steel, a wooden and a rubber ball around a tray. Open the chest and coins drop onto it.

  • Each ball vibrates on the side it is on
  • Each coin taps where it lands
  • Works with the stick or WASD too
Play
Adaptive triggersHD hapticsSpeaker

Pistol

R2 fires at the click halfway down. Three rounds, then a reload.

  • Click trigger
  • Recoil, then the hit on the target
  • Reload clicks on the controller speaker
Play
Adaptive triggersHD hapticsSpeaker

Machine gun

Hold R2 and the trigger vibrates as it fires, twelve rounds a second. If it overheats, the trigger locks until it cools.

  • Vibrating trigger
  • Overheat locks the trigger
Play
Adaptive triggersHD haptics

Bow

R2 gets heavier the further you pull. Release to shoot.

  • Resistance increases with the draw
  • Vibration at full draw

Melee

Fourteen melee moves against a straw dummy. Left and right on the D-pad change the move.

  • Hit timing comes from each move
  • Heavier moves vibrate longer

Frequently asked questions

Desktop Chrome and Edge. They support WebHID, which talks to the controller directly. Safari and Firefox do not.

HD haptics travel as audio, and browsers can only reach the controller audio over USB. On Bluetooth everything still works, with classic rumble instead.

Chrome hides audio device names until a media permission is granted. We ask once to find the DualSense output and close the stream immediately. Nothing is recorded.

v0.6Nov 2, 2026
NewImproved

Weather lab and Treasure tray

Rain was replaced by a weather lab with 12 types. The tray rolls balls when you tilt the controller, and drops coins from a chest. The playground now has two floors, combos and stronger hit vibration.

  • Device and vibration status always on screen
  • Vibration test in the menu
  • Mouse buttons for attack and R2
  • Fix: the hammer no longer turns into a sword on a heavy attack
  • Fix: removed a squeaky sound when firing

HD haptics working

The controller ignored audio haptics while a legacy rumble flag was set in its output report. Rumble is now only sent when HD haptics are off.

  • Adaptive triggers over WebHID
